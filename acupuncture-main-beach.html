Worth the short drive south

Main Beach sits right at the busier end of the Gold Coast strip, close to Tedder Avenue, the marina and the Broadwater. It's a wonderful place to live, but not always the easiest place to find a genuinely quiet hour to yourself.

Ānshén Acupuncture is a short drive south in Mermaid Beach: a calmer stretch of the coast, an unhurried appointment length, and a Chinese medicine assessment that considers your whole picture, not just today's symptom.

Why clients make the trip

  • Free, easy street parking, no searching for a spot
  • A quiet clinic away from the busier northern strip

Good to know

Common questions.

How far is the clinic from Main Beach?

Around 12 minutes by car via the Gold Coast Highway, traffic depending. We're at 4/2546 Gold Coast Hwy, Mermaid Beach.

Is there parking near the clinic?

Yes, street parking is available near the clinic and no bookings are required for parking.

Do you only see clients who live locally?

Not at all. We see clients from across the Gold Coast, including Main Beach, Surfers Paradise and Southport. Plenty of people find the short drive south worth it for an unhurried appointment.

Can I book an initial appointment from Main Beach?

Yes, a 90 minute initial acupuncture appointment is the usual starting point regardless of which suburb you're travelling from.

Ready when you are.

Appointments are available Tuesdays, 12:00pm to 5:00pm, a short drive south of Main Beach.
