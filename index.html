How it works

From first visit to ongoing care.

Initial consultation

A 90 minute first appointment: your health history, a Chinese medicine assessment and your first treatment.

Treatment

Acupuncture in a quiet room, with cupping, moxibustion or gua sha added where appropriate.

Ongoing care

Shorter follow-up sessions review your progress and keep your treatment plan on track.

Why Ānshén?
