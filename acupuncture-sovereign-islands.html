Good to know

Common questions.

How far is the clinic from Sovereign Islands?

Around 20 minutes by car, traffic depending. We're at 4/2546 Gold Coast Hwy, Mermaid Beach, south along the highway from Paradise Point.

Is the clinic private and discreet?

Yes. Appointments are one-on-one in a quiet treatment room, with a single appointment time per visit rather than an open, walk-in clinic feel.

Do you offer appointments outside of Tuesdays?

Appointments are currently offered on Tuesdays, 12:00pm to 5:00pm. Get in touch if you'd like to discuss timing.

What should I book for a first visit?

A 90 minute initial acupuncture appointment, which allows time for a full history, a Chinese medicine assessment and your first treatment.

Ready when you are.

Appointments are available Tuesdays, 12:00pm to 5:00pm, a short drive from Sovereign Islands.
